02 / THE HUMAN BEHIND IT

Different problems.
The same curiosity.

I enjoy taking an idea, understanding the problem behind it, and working through what it takes to build a useful solution. Development gives me room to think about both the experience someone sees and the systems that make it possible.

As a full-stack developer, I work across frontend and backend development. I care about how an application feels to use, how information moves through it, and how the pieces fit together. The aim is to make something that feels straightforward, even when the problem behind it is complicated.
